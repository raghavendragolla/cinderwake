## Notes for the tester

- Settings worth trying once: **Aim guide** (on/off), **Reduce motion**, **Score numbers**.
- The death screen shows Flame efficiency, Perfect dashes, dash quality tally,
  Rekindle used, synergies in play, and a next-goal line — use it to answer above.
- Pause (Esc / P) is always available; nothing interrupts combat.
- If anything crashes, freezes, or shows a wrong number, write the wave + what you
  were doing — that's a bug report, not feedback.
